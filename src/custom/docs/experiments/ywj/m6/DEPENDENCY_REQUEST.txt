   Evaluation-only runs with `main.py --ground-truth` compared
   /custom/estimated_pose against the debug /odom (an external monitor;
   mission code never reads /odom). Mission 6 only, default scenario:

   run    pos err median / p90 / max (m)   x bias median   |yaw err| while standing still
   diag2  0.21 / 0.28 / 0.98                +0.19           median 0.1 deg
   diag5  0.23 / 0.32 / 0.86                +0.22           median 0.6 deg
   diag7  0.29 / 0.32 / 0.63                +0.24           median 0.5 deg
   diag4  0.66 / 1.61 / 1.65                +0.56           median 10.8 deg

   - A constant +0.2..0.3 m x bias is present from the start pose (16,11).
   - Runs with large errors coincide with "[localizer] sensor reanchor":
     the rejected jump is dropped, but the robot's actual motion during the
     rejected interval is lost and the yaw error then persists (10-40 deg).
   - 12 of 20 mission-6 runs logged at least one reanchor, typically during
     in-place turns or right after the body contacted the ball.
   - /custom/estimated_pose publishing gaps of 0.4-1.2 s were measured by a
     read-only subscriber; ContinuousPose.stale (1.2 s) then raises
     "Stale localization" in Navigator.goto/face. Mission 6 now tolerates up
     to 4 s locally (mission_6.py `_pose`), but other missions still abort.

   Requested: absolute re-localization against the public map after a
   reanchor (or at least yaw correction from the room walls), and a pose
   publishing rate that keeps gaps below the 1.2 s stale threshold.
   Reproduction: see ../../ywj_m6_20261124.md, "Reproduce".

2. Head camera near-field visibility (sensors.yaml / walking policy)
   With camera pitch 15 deg the lowest image row reaches the floor ~1.6-2.0 m
   ahead of the pelvis while the G1 is stepping, but 3-4.9 m ahead about 1 s
   after it stops (the torso straightens). A floor ball closer than ~2 m is
   never visible; a standing robot loses it out to ~4 m. Mission 6 works
   around this by creeping backwards (-0.05 m/s) while observing.
   A larger camera pitch would help mission 6 but changes missions 2/4;
   please decide at integration level. No change requested unilaterally.
