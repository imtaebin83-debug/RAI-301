## Final changes

- Keep the common Navigator and continuous sensor pose. Bound the mission, check route/heading/approach failures and always stop, including on begin_mission errors. Use the requested planner colour with the permitted practice fallback.
- Require at least three consistent, advancing RGB/depth measurements. Project a private snapshot of exactly the paired immutable camera messages; do not reread live caches between validating and projecting. The previous implementation could race a camera update and discard a good pair. The regression test deliberately advances the live cache during projection and verifies snapshot isolation.
- Survey while stopped, then use continuous sensor-pose feedback for the measured cone approach. The short-step visual-reacquisition draft failed: a close-range capture showed the fixed camera looking above the cones. No new close-range image is required where the camera may not see them. Limit measured approach distance to 3.5 m, use speed 0.18 m/s, then verify estimated position after final rotation.
- Retain the baseline 0.08 m surface correction and published 0.75 m offset. This remains an approximation, not a measured radius. The final pose check is against the fresh sensor target, not hidden ground truth.
- Only cone() in vision.py gains an optional projected-frame argument. Shared frame decoding, generic observation and ball processing remain unchanged. No navigation, localisation, bringup, planner, sensor/walking settings, dependency list or other mission was changed.
