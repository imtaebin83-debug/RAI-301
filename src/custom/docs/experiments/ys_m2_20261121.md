## Changes and reasons

- `mission_2.py`: keep the required class/API and use the common Navigator. Bound the overall attempt, stop even if begin_mission fails, reject invalid command colours, and check heading/waypoint failures.
- `perception.py`: cone-only observer requires advancing RGB and depth timestamps, pairing within 80 ms, and at least three consistent measured positions. It rejects cached frames, invalid measurements, inconsistent clusters, and jumps to another track. It does not modify the shared frame decoder, ball detector, or generic observe method.
- `vision.py`: only cone() changes: it accepts an optional already-projected frame. Existing callers are compatible; ball processing is unchanged.
- Approach in at most 0.55 m steps with re-observation while the cone is visible. Finish the final at-most-0.70 m segment at 0.14 m/s using continuous sensor-derived pose feedback against the last fresh cone target. Check pose error again after the final turn. This final check is **not** a new camera detection or proof of ground-truth accuracy.
- Preserve the baseline 0.08 m surface correction (0.75 m published offset minus the previous 0.67 m surface offset). It remains an uncalibrated approximation, not an inferred cone radius.
