Navigator pose/goto/route_to/face/camera_to_world/stop signatures are preserved.
Walking velocity now scales the complete body vector before axis limits, fixing
the old independent clipping that could command 0.18m/s north for a westward
cliff-strip target. The supplied walking policy and velocity caps are unchanged.
estimated_pose and map->pelvis TF now carry the accepted LiDAR scan timestamp,
in the same simulated time domain as robot TF. The additive String topic
/custom/localization_status (version=1) provides valid/terminal/reason/quality,
accepted measurement_stamp, rejected_scans, recovery_count, and ICP metrics.
Navigator stops immediately on invalid health and waits at most 0.8s for fresh
sensor recovery in goto/face. Direct pose() still fails closed on stale data.
Older publishers without the optional status topic retain the receipt watchdog.

KISS map updates occur only after motion, IMU agreement, correspondence support,
residual and pelvis-transform checks. A rejected scan retains the last trusted
map and pose. No waypoint snapping, truth read, command dead reckoning or relaxed
jump limit is introduced. Translation always needs a successful scan alignment.
